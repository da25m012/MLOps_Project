Dataset — NASA CMAPSS FD001

The C-MAPSS (Commercial Modular Aero-Propulsion System Simulation) dataset contains run-to-failure data for turbofan jet engines. Subset FD001 has 100 training engines and 100 test engines, each with 21 sensor readings per cycle. 14 informative sensors are selected after removing constant-value channels. The LSTM Autoencoder is trained on early cycles (cycle ≤ 125) where engines are healthy, and flags late cycles (near failure) as anomalous.

100 training engines 14 sensors selected seq_len = 30 cycles threshold at p95
